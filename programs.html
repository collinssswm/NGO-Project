Our Programs

Six focused areas of work, one shared goal: thriving communities.

Education for All

Access to quality education is the foundation of everything we do. Our Education for All programme provides scholarships and bursaries for primary and secondary school learners, distributes learning materials, runs remedial tutoring centres, and trains teachers in child-centred pedagogical methods.

We have supported over 3,500 students since 2018, with a 92% retention rate among scholarship recipients — proving that financial support, combined with mentorship, keeps children in school.

3 500+ students supported 12 tutoring centres Scholarships & bursaries Teacher training Learning materials

Community Health

Our mobile health clinics travel to remote areas monthly, providing free consultations, vaccinations, maternal and antenatal care, and nutrition counselling. We also run community health worker training programmes that equip local volunteers with basic first-aid, disease prevention, and referral skills.

Since launch, our health programme has conducted more than 28,000 consultations and reduced under-5 mortality rates in target communities by 18%.

28 000+ consultations Mobile clinics Maternal health Vaccination drives 120 CHW trained
🌾

Sustainable Livelihoods

Economic insecurity is one of the biggest barriers to well-being. Our Livelihoods programme provides vocational skills training in tailoring, carpentry, welding, and digital skills; connects graduates to micro-finance loans; and runs a mentorship network with local business owners.

For smallholder farmers we offer agro-training on climate-smart practices, group savings (VSLA), and market-linkage support — helping families move beyond subsistence.

800+ trainees graduated Vocational training Micro-finance Agro-training Market linkages
💧

Clean Water Access

Safe water is a human right. HopeReach has drilled 18 boreholes, installed solar-powered water pumps, and constructed rainwater-harvesting systems serving over 15,000 people who previously relied on unsafe water sources.

We pair infrastructure with community-led Water Users' Associations (WUAs) that manage and maintain systems locally, ensuring long-term sustainability. WASH training in schools reaches an additional 4,000 children annually.

18 boreholes drilled 15 000 people served Solar-powered pumps WASH training Community WUAs
👩‍👧

Women Empowerment

Gender equality is both a goal and a pathway. Our Women Empowerment programme provides leadership development, legal literacy, GBV prevention training, and business development support exclusively for women and girls aged 15+.

Our Women's Savings and Loan Associations (WSLAs) have disbursed over KES 12 million in micro-loans, helping women start and grow businesses, pay school fees, and invest in their families' futures.

1 200 women enrolled Leadership training Legal literacy GBV prevention KES 12M in micro-loans
🌍

Environment & Climate

Communities on the frontline of climate change need practical solutions. HopeReach has planted 50,000 trees across degraded land, introduced fuel-efficient cookstoves in 2,000 homes, and established community-managed waste collection and recycling cooperatives.

Climate adaptation workshops help farmers diversify crops, improve soil health, and adopt water-conservation techniques — building resilience against increasingly unpredictable weather patterns.

50 000 trees planted 2 000 eco-cookstoves Waste cooperatives Climate adaptation Soil & water conservation
